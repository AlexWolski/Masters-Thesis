\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

In this work, we presented the CSG-CRN architecture and demonstrated its ability to reconstruct and refine 3D shapes using cascaded refinement. We show that CSG-CRN is capable of achieving higher reconstruction accuracy than related works in the domain. We proved the efficacy of cascaded refinement when applied to 3D reconstruction. And we provide an example of a real-world use case.

There are several areas of improvement remaining with the CSG-CRN implementation that can be addressed in future work. Since the model has a fixed output size once trained, each primitive may not be contributing to the reconstruction. A stop output would allow the model to dynamically control the number of primitives needed for a target shape and simplify the output. The generalizability and output scope of CSG-CRN can be enhanced by dynamically changing the section of a target shape fed to the network as the reconstruction progresses. And the general capabilities of the model can be enhanced by updating the encoder and decoder networks.
