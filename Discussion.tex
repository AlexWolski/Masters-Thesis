\chapter{Discussion}
\label{chap:discussion}

This section summarizes the main insights gained from the \nameref{chap:experiments_and_results} chapter. Our results from the \nameref{sec:prims_per_cascade_experiment} experiment are summarized in figure~\ref{fig:prims_per_cascade_comparison}. This result proves our hypothesis that cascaded refinement networks are an effective strategy for structured 3D reconstruction tasks. The same network architecture performs significantly better in a cascaded refinement configuration as opposed to the traditional end-to-end feedforward configuration.

\begin{figure}[!htb]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{Images/Cascade Configuration Figure.png}
	\caption{Bar graph of the data in table~\ref{tab:input_size_comparison}. Lower is better for CD and EMD. Higher is better for IoU. The cascaded refinement configuration format is~$\text{primitives per cascade} \times \text{number of cascades}$.}
	\label{fig:prims_per_cascade_comparison}
\end{figure}

The \nameref{sec:prior_work_comparison} experiment further demonstrates the viability of cascaded refinement. Figure~\ref{fig:prior_work_seen_comparison} shows the CSG-CRN model ranking first against competing models in all accuracy metrics for average reconstruction quality of seen shape categories. CSG-CRN has a big lead in EMD and IoU metrics with the SuperDec model trailing closely behind on the CD metric. We attribute the high performance of CSG-CRN to the cascaded refinement strategy considering the encoder and decoder networks are not novel and are outdated compared to recent related works.

\begin{figure}[!htb]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{Images/Prior Work Seen Comparison.png}
	\caption{Bar graph of the data in table~\ref{tab:prior_work_seen_comparison}. Lower is better for CD and EMD. Higher is better for IoU.}
	\label{fig:prior_work_seen_comparison}
\end{figure}

Figure \ref{fig:prior_work_unseen_comparison} shows the performance of CSG-CRN on unseen shape categories. CSG-CRN and SuperDec perform very similarly to each other, with both outperforming the remaining works by a large margin. This result demonstrates that the cascaded refinement strategy enables strongly generalizability that rivals the current state of the art.
